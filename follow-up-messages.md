# Consent-based follow-up

These messages go **only** to people who have (a) requested a health check and ticked the consent box, or (b) are existing customers or contacts who have agreed to hear from Tobb. Do not send them to purchased lists or people who have not agreed. Have the wording checked before use.

## A. Request form wording

**Fields:** name, business name, email, website address.

**Consent checkbox (not pre-ticked):**
> I agree that Tobb Technologies may email me my Website Health Check report and follow up about it. I can ask to stop at any time.

**Below the form:**
> Tobb Technologies, [email address], [phone number]. We use your details only to prepare your report and contact you about it. [Link to privacy notice]

## B. Message 1: confirmation (send immediately)

**Subject:** We have your Website Health Check request

Hi [first name],

Thank you for requesting a Website Health Check for [website]. We will send your report within 3 working days.

If you have a question in the meantime, reply to this email.

Kind regards,
[sender name], Tobb Technologies
[email address] | [phone number]

## E. Discovery-call questions (20 minutes)

1. What does your business do, and who are your customers?
2. How do customers find you today?
3. What is the main thing you want your website or systems to do better?
4. What have you tried already, and what happened?
5. What would success look like in six months?
6. Who else needs to be involved in a decision?
7. What are your time and budget limits?

**After the call:** send a short summary of what you heard and agree the next step. If they say no or stop replying, stop contacting them.
