# Proposal: Proof First

A consent-led client acquisition package for Tobb Technologies.

## 1. Opportunity

Small businesses often know they need a stronger online presence but struggle to act on it. A 2019 GoDaddy survey of 250 South African small business owners found that time, budget and online knowledge were significant barriers to getting online (BusinessTech, 2019). A 2018 Snupit survey found that most small businesses relied on word of mouth, and fewer than half had a website (ITWeb, 2018).

This is an opening for Tobb Technologies. Its services (web platforms, databases, front-end work and redevelopment) address exactly these gaps. The challenge is that a prospect who has never worked with Tobb has little reason to trust it yet. This proposal gives Tobb a way to **show proof of its work first** and make the first step easy, free and low-risk.

## 2. Justification

### 2.1 What external sources say

- Buyers use evidence from vendors. In a 2015 survey, 86% of B2B respondents said vendor-focused content such as case studies influenced their purchase decisions, and 83% said content that built a business case or showed clear ROI was somewhat or very important (Demand Gen Report, 2015).
- Trust is personal as well as rational. In a 2024 survey of 3,528 B2B buyers, 52% said personal drivers influenced their purchase decisions, up from 35% in 2021, and the biggest factor was whether they felt safe signing a contract with the vendor. 68% said many B2B brands sound the same (Nanji, 2024).
- Understanding the buyer matters. In a 2020 survey of 528 buyers and sellers, 71% said a thorough discovery of their needs was highly influential, and 66% said a clear ROI case was (MarketingProfs, 2021).
- Vendor case studies have limits. A summary of 2019 TrustRadius research reported that just over 20% of surveyed technology buyers used vendor case studies, and ranked them 11th of 15 for trustworthiness because they are seen as hand-picked (CustomerThink, n.d.).
- Cold electronic outreach carries legal risk. POPIA section 69 requires consent before unsolicited direct marketing by electronic communication, and an opt-out option alone does not count as consent (Information Regulator, 2024; Information Regulator, 2026). Commentary from a South African law firm notes that this applies to B2B marketing and to personalised LinkedIn pitches (MJK Inc, n.d.).

### 2.2 Own observations (from my experience at Tobb)

- On the Zonke redevelopment, our team of three added courses, contests and blogs to an existing Laravel/PHP site, after a senior review moved the project from a rebuild to customising the existing codebase.
- Zonke connects clients with freelancers, and its database links users, jobs and proposals. It is a realistic example of the kind of platform Tobb builds.

### 2.3 What the evidence does not show

Several surveys are global, B2B-focused or dated (2015 to 2019), and the South African samples are small. The Snupit and GoDaddy figures describe barriers, not what would win clients for Tobb. None of this proves that this campaign will convert, so the success measures below are designed to test it.

### 2.4 How the design responds

- To the trust gap, it leads with a real project and invites the prospect to check it.
- To skepticism of hand-picked case studies, it includes real results and a client voice, and offers a free health check that gives value before any sales conversation.
- To the legal risk, it uses inbound requests with consent, rather than cold emails.

## 3. Proposed solution

| Component | Purpose | File |
|---|---|---|
| Digital brochure | One-page overview for small-business owners, built around the Zonke case study | `marketing-assets/digital-brochure.md` |
| Free Website Health Check | A 12-point review that gives a prospect value and shows Tobb's expertise | `marketing-assets/website-health-check.md` |
| LinkedIn campaign | Five posts that build awareness and send people to the request form | `marketing-assets/linkedin-campaign.md` |
| Consent-based follow-up | Consent wording, three messages and discovery questions | `marketing-assets/follow-up-messages.md` |

**How it works:** posts and the brochure attract interest, a prospect requests a free health check and ticks a consent box, Tobb delivers the check, and then follows up with a short sequence ending in a discovery call.

## 4. Implementation

**What Tobb needs**

- Permission to publish the Zonke case study, and its real results.
- A request form with a consent checkbox (any form tool will do).
- A LinkedIn page [confirm one exists] and one person with 2 to 3 hours a week.
- A sign-off from someone senior on claims and compliance.

**Timeline**

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Week 0 | Get permission, add real results, fill placeholders, set up the form |
| Weeks 1 to 2 | Publish the brochure page and the five LinkedIn posts |
| Weeks 2 to 6 | Deliver health checks within 3 working days, send follow-ups, hold discovery calls |
| Week 6 | Review results and decide whether to continue, change or stop |

## 5. Benefits to Tobb

- **Credibility:** a published case study and a free, practical first step.
- **Better leads:** people who ask for a health check have shown interest and given consent.
- **Lower risk:** the process avoids unsolicited electronic marketing.
- **Reusable assets:** the brochure, checklist and posts can be reused and extended with new case studies.
- **Low cost:** the main cost is staff time.

## 6. Success measures

The targets below are starting points to agree with Tobb, not forecasts.

| Measure | How it is measured | Starting target (6 weeks) |
|---|---|---|
| Post reach and engagement | LinkedIn page analytics | Track weekly; set the baseline in week 1 |
| Health check requests | Count of form submissions | 10 |
| Discovery calls held | Tally of calls | 4 |
| Proposals sent | Tally | 2 |
| New clients won | Tally | 1 |
| Opt-outs or complaints | Tally | 0 |

Review at weeks 2, 4 and 6. If requests are low, test a new post angle or audience. If requests are high but calls are low, improve the follow-up.

## 7. Risks and limits

- **Permission and results:** the case study depends on Tobb sharing real outcomes.
- **Capacity:** health checks take time, so Tobb may need to cap requests per week.
- **Legal:** I am not a lawyer, so Tobb should have the consent wording checked before use.
- **Small sample:** six weeks may be too short to see a client won.
