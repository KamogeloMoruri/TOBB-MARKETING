# Proof First: a consent-led client acquisition package for Tobb Technologies

Business Growth & Marketing Challenge, Friday 30 October 2026

## Overview

ZONKE.ME is a ready-to-use package that helps Tobb Technologies win new clients by leading with evidence of its own work. It turns the Zonke redevelopment project into a case study, offers prospects a free **Website Health Check**, and follows up only with people who have asked to hear from Tobb.

## Purpose

Tobb needs a simple, low-cost way to reach new clients and give them a reason to trust a company they have not worked with before. This package gives Tobb the assets to do that without a marketing budget, and keeps all outreach compliant with South Africa's POPIA direct-marketing rules.

## Target audience

Owners and managers of small and growing businesses that have no website, an outdated one, or manual processes that a simple system could replace.

## Business value

- Gives Tobb a credible, repeatable story (the Zonke case study) to show prospects.
- Creates a low-risk first step for a prospect (the free health check) that also reveals what they need.
- Builds a list of people who have agreed to be contacted, so follow-up is lawful and welcome.
- Costs staff time only: about 2 to 3 hours a week for six weeks.

## How Tobb could use it

1. Confirm permission to publish the Zonke story and add its real results.
2. Fill in the `[placeholders]` in the marketing assets (contact details, results, links).
3. Post the five LinkedIn posts over two weeks, each pointing to the health check request form.
4. Deliver each health check, then follow up using the consent-based message sequence.
5. Track results against the success measures in `proposal.md` and review at weeks 2, 4 and 6.
