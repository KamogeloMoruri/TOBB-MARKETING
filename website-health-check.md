# Free Website Health Check

A 12-point review Tobb completes for a prospect within 3 working days of a request. Each point is rated **Green** (good), **Amber** (could improve) or **Red** (fix first).

## The checklist

### Findability
1. **Mobile friendly:** the site is readable and usable on a phone.
2. **Page title and description:** the home page says clearly what the business does and where.
3. **Contact details:** phone, email and location are easy to find on every page.

### Trust
4. **Secure connection:** the site loads over HTTPS with no browser warnings.
5. **About and proof:** there is a clear About page and at least one testimonial or example of work.
6. **Up to date:** content, prices and opening details are current.

### Quality
7. **Speed:** the home page loads quickly on a mobile connection.
8. **Working links and forms:** no broken links, and the contact form sends and is answered.
9. **Readable and accessible:** text is large enough, has good contrast, and images have descriptions.

### Turning visitors into enquiries
10. **Clear next step:** each page tells the visitor what to do (call, request a quote, book).
11. **Easy contact options:** click-to-call or messaging is available on mobile.
12. **Looked after:** the site has backups and security updates handled by someone.
